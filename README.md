# Sonic Candle - Video for your music

[![Sonic Candle CI](https://github.com/acromere/sonic/actions/workflows/ci.yml/badge.svg)](https://github.com/acromere/sonic/actions/workflows/ci.yml)

"Given a music file, generates a video file with a visualization of frequency spectrum bars generated for the music. Outputs high-quality, rendered video. Intended for publishing your own music on YouTube and stuff =)"

[web](https://soniccandle.com), [discord](https://discord.gg/MANggMn2aR)

## History

Sonic Candle was created by Ryan Schroeder. The goal was to create an application to make beautiful high quality videos for artists wanting to make music videos. A few months after Ryan began development, Chris Soderquist joined in. The two are now working to develop a good and free alternative to expensive renderers so that musicians can spend their time worrying about the music, and not the money.
